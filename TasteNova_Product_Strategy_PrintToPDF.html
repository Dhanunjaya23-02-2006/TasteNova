TasteNova (HomeChef) - Product Strategy & Business Documentation

This document outlines the core business strategy, problem statements, competitive landscape, pricing models, and operational terms for TasteNova (a home-chef food delivery platform).

1. Product Overview

TasteNova is a hyperlocal food delivery platform designed exclusively to connect independent Home Chefs with customers looking for authentic, healthy, and hygienic home-cooked meals. Unlike traditional food delivery apps that aggregate commercial restaurants, TasteNova empowers homemakers, amateur cooks, and culinary enthusiasts to monetize their cooking skills straight from their own kitchens.

2. Problem Statements

TasteNova addresses two primary sets of problems in the current food ecosystem:

For Consumers:

  • Health and Hygiene: Relying on restaurant food (Swiggy/Zomato) for daily meals is often unhealthy, oily, and causes long-term health issues.
  • Nostalgia & Authenticity: Working professionals and students living away from home crave authentic, regional home-cooked food ("Ghar ka khana") which commercial restaurants fail to replicate.
  • Daily Affordability: Ordering from restaurants daily is expensive due to high taxes, packaging charges, and restaurant markups. Finding reliable, high-quality, and affordable daily tiffin services is difficult.

For Home Chefs (Supply Side):

  • High Barrier to Entry: Setting up a restaurant or a commercial cloud kitchen requires significant capital, licensing, and marketing efforts.
  • Lack of Reach: Homemakers and passionate cooks have limited ways to reach a broader audience beyond their immediate neighbors and friends.
  • Logistics & Delivery: Managing packaging, delivery logistics, and payments is complex for an individual cooking from home.

3. How TasteNova is Different from Swiggy & Zomato

Feature TasteNova (HomeChef) Swiggy / Zomato
Kitchen Type Unregistered, authentic Home Kitchens (FSSAI basic). Commercial Restaurants & Cloud Kitchens.
Food Quality Everyday, healthy, low-oil, authentic home-cooked meals. Indulgent, fast food, heavy curries, commercial taste.
Order Type Heavy focus on Pre-orders and Subscriptions (weekly/monthly). Purely On-demand (impulse buying).
Customization High. Chefs can customize based on specific dietary or regional requests. Low. Standardized restaurant menus.
Target Audience Bachelors, working professionals needing daily meals. Anyone looking for an occasional treat or quick bite.

4. Competitors

Direct Competitors

  • Homefoodi / FoodCloud / Oota Box: These are direct competitors operating in the home-chef aggregator space in India, though market penetration remains regional.
  • Local Unorganized Tiffin Services: The biggest traditional competitors. They rely on WhatsApp groups, pamphlets, and word-of-mouth.

Indirect Competitors

  • Swiggy & Zomato: Though they focus on restaurants, they offer "homestyle" categories and cloud kitchens.
  • EatClub (Box8): Offers affordable, standardized meals.
  • Zepto / Blinkit (Quick Commerce): Offering ready-to-eat/frozen meals and instant groceries.

5. Pricing Models (The ₹100, ₹199, ₹299 Tiers)

Tier 1: The Everyday Essential (₹99 - ₹120)

  • Target: Students, young professionals looking for daily sustenance.
  • Offering: Standard Veg Thali / Tiffin. (e.g., 4 Roti, 1 Sabzi, Dal, Rice, Salad).
  • Strategy: High volume, low margin. Best suited for subscription models.

Tier 2: The Premium Homestyle (₹189 - ₹220)

  • Target: Working professionals wanting better variety and weekend meals.
  • Offering: Non-Veg Thali, Regional Specialties.
  • Strategy: Medium volume, healthy margin. Designed for single orders and pre-orders.

Tier 3: The Gourmet / Festive Spread (₹289 - ₹350+)

  • Target: Families, weekend group orders, festival specials.
  • Offering: Premium ingredients, elaborate meals (e.g., Biryani, Festive Thalis).
  • Strategy: Low volume, high margin. Requires advanced pre-ordering (24 hours).

6. Revenue Streams

  1. Commission on Orders: Charging a flat 10% to 15% commission on every order processed through the platform.
  2. Customer Subscription Plans (TasteNova Pro): Customers pay a monthly fee (e.g., ₹149/month) for benefits like Zero Delivery Fees.
  3. Delivery Fees: Charged to the customer based on distance.
  4. Promoted Listings (Ads): Chefs can pay a small fee to appear at the top of the search results.
  5. Packaging Sales (B2B): Selling standardized, eco-friendly, branded packaging materials to home chefs.

7. Terms & Documentation

Chef Onboarding Documentation

  • FSSAI Registration: Basic registration for petty food business operators is mandatory.
  • KYC Documents: Aadhar Card, PAN Card, and Bank Account details.
  • Kitchen Hygiene Video/Photos: Mandatory upload during onboarding.

Legal Terms & Policies

  • Hygiene & Quality Standard Policy: Adherence to food safety guidelines. Unlisting of chefs if ratings drop below 3.5/5.
  • Cancellation & Refund Policy: Strict guidelines for on-demand vs. pre-orders to protect chefs from wastage.
  • Payout Terms: Bi-weekly settlements (T+3 or T+4).
  • User Data Privacy: Standard privacy policy for order fulfillment.
